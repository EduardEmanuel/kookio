About Kookio

Kookio is a culinary platform that helps you cook with what you already have. Instead of starting from a recipe and shopping for missing ingredients, you start from your pantry — Kookio surfaces dishes you can make right now and flags what's about to expire.

What it does

Add your ingredients to the pantry, browse recipes filtered by what you can cook today, plan the week ahead, and generate a shopping list from the gaps. Cooking a recipe decrements the matching pantry items automatically.

Project status

Kookio is an open-source project created as an academic proof-of-concept and freely available to use. It is under active development.
